CASE-001 · SYNTHETIC TEST DATA

SCS Insurance AI: the most important capability is not guessing an underwriting answer.

This case tests whether health data can be structured, whether decisions can be traced to evidence, and whether the system stops for human review when information is insufficient.

Business Problem

Non-standard underwriting information is fragmented.

Health metrics, medical history, disclosure questions, product rules and real underwriting outcomes come from different sources. The challenge is turning them into inspectable facts and decision paths.

Boundary

Decision support, not formal underwriting.

AI inference < product rules < real underwriting outcomes. When evidence conflicts, real underwriting results take precedence and trigger review.

Workflow
01InputHealth facts
02StructureNormalized variables
03Rule MatchDisclosure / product rules
04EvidenceFact + rule + source
05ReviewHuman triggers
06OutputDecision support
